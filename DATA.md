# Data: what is included, and how to rebuild what is not

## Included

- **Arithmetic items.** These are generated by the code: paper 4's M items, and every a * b with 11 ≤ a, b ≤ 99 that has a four-digit product.
- **Item and pair id lists.** For example `*/res/items.json`, `pairs*.json` and `pool_clean.jsonl`.
- **Per-arm summaries.** These are the `*_summary.json` files under `study/<arm>/res/`.

## Withheld, and how to rebuild

| File | Why it is withheld | How to rebuild |
|---|---|---|
| `code/data/calibration.json`, `code/data/items.json` (and their copies in `model2/`) | These are paper 6's shared data files. They contain data from repositories without a licence; see paper 6's DATA.md. Paper 8 uses only their D3 part, paper 4's 240 arithmetic M items, but `specs.py` reads both files at import. | Run paper 6's `make_items.py` (github.com/Somebodyhere101/direction-validity-battery, `code/`) with the sources it lists. |
| `code/data/items_d8.json` (and its copy in `model2/`) | ARC test questions, Challenge and Easy (Clark et al. 2018, arXiv:1803.05457; CC BY-SA 4.0). They are used only by the pilot's positive control and the tuned-lens fit. They are withheld to keep dataset text out of this release; they can be rebuilt. | Run paper 6's `make_items_d8.py` (same repository) from the ARC test set. |
| `*.npz` | Hidden-state arrays from the runs. | The `clean` and `run` stages recompute them. |
| `selfreport/res/tuned_lens.pt`, `model2/selfreport/res/tuned_lens.pt` | The fitted tuned lenses, 450 MB each. | `run_sr.py fitlens` rebuilds them from the frozen item choice (seed 5). A rebuild on other hardware may differ in the last bits. |
| `p8.ps1` | A deployment script for the original desktop GPU. It contains machine-specific addresses and paths and is not needed to reproduce the study. | Not needed. Run `code/run_laptop.py` instead. |
| `DEEPER.md` | AI-generated planning notes. They are not evidence. The frozen `selfreport2/PREDICTIONS.md` refers to them. | Not needed. |

Because these files are withheld, their lines in the `PREDICTIONS*.sha256` files cannot be verified from the release.

## Per-item results

`results-per-item.zip` is on Zenodo, not in git. It holds every per-item record of every arm: clean outcomes, patched rows, report logits, and lens and arrival tables.

The result files store only ids, digits, settle layers, logits, KL values and verdict inputs; they hold no model-written or prompt text. The release filter, which drops listed text keys and any other string longer than 40 characters, found nothing to remove (`study/strip_report.json`).
